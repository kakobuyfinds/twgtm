How to order with OrientDig

  1. Open the source listing. Select a catalog entry to see its current details on HipoBuyFind. Open Buy Link and choose OrientDig if it appears among the available platforms.
  2. Check the exact variant. Confirm the color, size, seller listing and price in the agent’s order form. Compare the seller’s measurements with an item you own that fits well.
  3. Review warehouse photos. When photos for your order are available, check the variant and visible condition. If you see a mismatch, check the agent’s exchange or return options before shipping.
  4. Compare parcel costs. Use the destination, packed weight and dimensions to compare available shipping lines. Review the total before paying for shipment.

Quality control checklist

QC stands for quality control. Use photos of your warehouse item to check details that matter to your order.

  • Shoes: Compare both shoes, check the size label and inspect the toe, heel and outsole. If sizing is uncertain, request an insole measurement if available.
  • T-shirts and hoodies: Check print placement, seams and sleeve length. Compare the flat chest width and body length with a garment you already own.
  • Jackets: Check the zipper, cuffs and lining. Compare sleeve length and chest width with a jacket that fits over your usual layers.
  • Bags: Inspect straps, hardware and stitching around attachment points. Verify the bag dimensions and check whether removable straps are included with the selected variant.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a directory of product finds that helps you compare listings before opening a seller or shopping-agent link. This edition uses product entries from HipoBuyFind.

What can I search for?

Search by item name, category words or a HipoBuyFind item number. You can combine the search field with a category filter and sort matching results by CNY price.

How do I estimate what I will pay?

Start with the current item price in the order form, then add any domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees and applicable taxes shown for your order and destination.

Where do I see QC photos for my order?

Check the warehouse or order details in your shopping-agent account after the item arrives. Photo availability depends on the agent and the order.
